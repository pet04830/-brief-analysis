  <h1>Formatting Analysis</h1>

  <p>
    For this document, I used HTML to turn my original essay about Maya Angelou’s
    <em>“Caged Bird”</em> into a more organized and visually accessible webpage.
    I used a clear <strong>title</strong> at the top of the document and added
    section headings for the introduction, audience and message, rhetorical
    strategies, and conclusion. These headings make it easier for readers to
    navigate the document and quickly understand what each section is about.
  </p>

  <p>
    I also used paragraph tags to separate my ideas and make the essay easier to
    read on a screen. I included <strong>bold text</strong> to emphasize the main
    purpose of the poem and used <em>italics</em> for important terms such as
    metaphor, allusion, and repetition. These formatting choices help draw
    attention to important ideas without changing the actual argument of the
    essay.
  </p>

  <p>
    One of the main formatting choices I made was adding a bulleted list to the
    rhetorical strategies section. The list breaks down Angelou’s use of allusion,
    metaphor, and repetition, making those three ideas easier to identify. I also
    included a quotation in a blockquote so that Angelou’s words stand out from
    the rest of the analysis.
  </p>

  <p>
    I added an image of Maya Angelou near the beginning of the document to give
    readers a visual connection to the author. I also included a link to the
    Poetry Foundation so readers can find additional information about the poem.
    I used basic CSS to control the spacing, font, background, headings, image,
    and overall appearance
